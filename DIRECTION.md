# Frontend direction contract

THESIS: A precise product shop, with the collection and buying controls doing the work. No invented promotional claims or product photography.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white surfaces, graphite header, restrained blue actions, Manrope typography, compact 6px controls, open product shelves and flat administrative tables.

STORY: Browse and search the store, inspect a product, add it to a bag, review current totals and complete a clearly simulated payment. Product management is a separate destination.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark navigation with Store left, Shop and Manage products centre, bag right. A 58px heading, supporting sentence and wide search field sit above a category index and three-column product grid. Mobile stacks the search and uses a native category select.

FORM: User selected the monochrome direction. The implemented interface and DESIGN.md define the maintained reference.

FINISH: Maintain DESIGN.md and verify the interface with the browser tests.
